Chocolate Hills

"The Chocolate Hills are a world-famous geological formation located in the central region of Bohol Island, Philippines. Spanning an area of over 50 square kilometers, this breathtaking natural landscape consists of a vast expanse of symmetrical, cone-shaped mounds numbering between 1,260 and 1,776 individual hills. Declared the country's third National Geological Monument, the hills are covered in a resilient grass that transforms from a vibrant green during the wet season into a uniform cocoa-brown color during the dry season, making the landscape resemble an endless spread of chocolate confections.
